## Scope

Public static tourist guide for Fusagasugá. Visitor mode: Experience. The source Laravel interface is replaced with one responsive Angular app while retaining its public navigation and destination information.

## Direction contract

THESIS: FusaTour is a living route index, not a generic travel catalogue. It refuses the interchangeable hero-plus-card-grid pattern by making every route and destination read as a marked stop in one continuous civic guide.

OWN-WORLD: The interface uses deep garden green, sunlit stone, saturated terracotta and night-indigo. Strong square route markers, measured rules and a type-led editorial rhythm hold real photography in asymmetrical destination bands. Dark mode becomes the same guide after dusk: indigo ground, warm stone text and luminous terracotta waypoints.

STORY: A visitor arrives at the city, chooses a way to explore—by category, activity, ranking or a direct search—and opens a field note with story, gallery, map, associated activities and the published catalogue rating.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width city image anchors the right half of the opening while the left is a tall route marker with the FusaTour title, city promise, search and a direct exploration action. A compact vertical index of the three real categories begins at the lower edge and immediately exposes the catalog.

FORM: Municipal route board with editorial field notes, candidate 5 of the grounded direction list. seed:5f7c6c1bbc2cb077. It keeps the steel-partition challenger’s structural discipline as a donated principle—each destination has a deliberate, clearly bounded place—without borrowing its glass aesthetic.
